# Email Studio

This studio plans and writes the email launch campaign for our book, **The AI Agent Factory, second edition** (by Panaversity). Emails go only to a practice email server, never to real people.

## Folders

- **brand/**: how the brand sounds, including the voice guide.
- **leads/**: made-up practice customers to send emails to.
- **campaigns/**: one folder per campaign, with its plan, emails and group versions.
- **notes/**: research notes and decisions, such as which skills we chose.

Reference files in the root folder: `brand-brief.md` (facts about the book), `email_studio.md` (the assignment steps) and `book-prompts.md` (prompts adapted for the book).

## Rules

- Write in plain English.
- Never send email to real people.
- Ask me before deleting anything.

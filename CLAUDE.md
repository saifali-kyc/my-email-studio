# Rise & Crumb email studio

This studio plans and writes email campaigns for Rise & Crumb, a small, made-up neighbourhood bakery. Brand rules live in `brand/`, made-up practice customers in `leads/`, and one folder per campaign in `campaigns/`. Write in plain English. Emails go only to a practice email server, never to real people: send only to Resend's test addresses (ending in `@resend.dev`) and to my own address in `MY_EMAIL`. Ask me before deleting anything.

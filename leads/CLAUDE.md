# Leads folder rules

These rules apply to this folder only.

- **Everyone here is made up.** The people in `leads.csv` are practice customers, not real people.
- **Never add real people or real email addresses.** No real names of people you know, no real inboxes, and no addresses copied from anywhere else.
- **Every row needs a consent value.** The `consent` column must never be empty. If you don't know how someone joined, don't add them.
- **Never change the @example.com addresses.** Every email address ends in `@example.com`. That domain is reserved and can never receive mail, so no real person is ever emailed.
- **Use only these group values:** `weekend-bakers`, `coffee-lovers`.
- **Keep the columns exactly as they are:** `first_name,last_name,email,group,joined_on,consent`.

## Groups

- **weekend-bakers**: home bakers who come in on Saturday mornings for ideas, flour and a loaf to compare with their own.
- **coffee-lovers**: regulars who drop in on weekday mornings for a coffee and something sweet to go with it.

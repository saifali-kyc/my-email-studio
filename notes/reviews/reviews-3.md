# Customer reviews: The AI Agent Factory, Second Edition (part 3 of 6)

Made-up practice data. These reviewers are not real people.

---

**41. Sana I. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
"The Roles This Book Trains" is a brilliant way to start. I knew exactly which parts to focus on. Arrived early, too.

**42. Elliot M. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Good read, but the crash course instructions lost me around the third step. I had to watch videos elsewhere to catch up.

**43. Noor A. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
The Thesis is the strongest chapter. I just wish the book spent longer on it before moving on to the practical parts.

**44. Callum J. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
Slow shipping and no updates. When it came, the plastic wrap was split and the cover had a crease.

**45. Isabel G. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
"Selling as a Vertical FDE" changed how I talk to clients. Practical, kind and very direct. Love it.

**46. Viktor P. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
I liked it, but there's too little on certifications. I wanted a clear path, and it only sketches one.

**47. Maya D. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Easy to read in short bursts. The Glossary is handy. The setup steps could be laid out more clearly, with one action per line.

**48. Oscar F. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
"The Operating Layer" was a lightbulb moment. I've already quoted it in two team meetings.

**49. Imani T. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**
Pages 40 to 56 were printed upside down. Returning it. Customer service was slow to respond.

**50. Ryan K. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Really good. Took eight days to arrive, but the packing was solid. The Preface is short and powerful.

**51. Ana L. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
The essays are great, but the crash courses read like a different book. The instructions are confusing and skip steps.

**52. Faisal M. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
"Getting Paid as a Vertical FDE" alone is worth the price. It's honest about how the work actually gets paid.

**53. Jess H. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
I wanted much more on "The Ecosystem". It felt like a quick tour when I wanted a deep dive.

**54. Dev S. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Smart and readable. My copy arrived with a small dent in the corner, but nothing serious.

**55. Clara B. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
"How to Learn from This Book" is a gift. It made me slow down and actually practise. I'm learning more because of it.

**56. Mohammed E. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Good ideas, but I got stuck on the setup. The book says to "connect the tool" without saying where to click.

**57. Poppy W. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
"What You Carry In" is lovely and reassuring. I'd have liked more examples from people outside tech.

**58. Hugo R. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
Two weeks to arrive and the box looked like it had been stood on. The book inside survived, just about.

**59. Layla N. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Clear, warm and useful. "AI Is Non-Negotiable" answered every doubt I had. Would buy again.

**60. Sean O. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
I enjoyed the arguments, but the Glossary is too short. Several terms I looked up weren't there.

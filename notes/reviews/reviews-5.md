# Customer reviews: The AI Agent Factory, Second Edition (part 5 of 6)

Made-up practice data. These reviewers are not real people.

---

**81. Alice M. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
"Getting Paid as a Vertical FDE" gave me the confidence to set my own rates. Practical and kind.

**82. Omar F. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
The setup steps in the crash courses are too brief. "Install and connect" is not enough instruction for a beginner.

**83. Georgia K. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Loved the Preface. The book arrived with a scuffed back cover, but the pages were fine.

**84. Ravi T. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
Far too little on "The Ecosystem". I wanted a full map of the tools, and it only scratches the surface.

**85. Martha S. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
"The Roles This Book Trains" is genius. It helped me explain my new job to my family.

**86. Nikolai V. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Shipping took two weeks. The essays are excellent, but the crash courses confused me.

**87. Amina O. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
"What You Carry In" is beautifully argued. I'd love a follow-up chapter with more stories.

**88. Josh P. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
The box arrived split open. Luckily the book was inside, but the corners were badly bent.

**89. Tara R. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Clear and honest. "AI Is Non-Negotiable" is the chapter I wish my whole team would read.

**90. Ahmed L. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Good ideas, but the setup instructions use different names for the same thing. It took me a while to realise they meant the same tool.

**91. Holly N. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
"Selling as a Vertical FDE" is short but packed. I wanted more on handling objections from clients.

**92. Wei Z. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Fast delivery, sturdy packaging, and a great read. "The Operating Layer" was my highlight.

**93. Kayla B. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**
Still waiting after four weeks. The tracking says delivered, but it isn't here. Very poor.

**94. Rahul G. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
The Thesis is excellent. The Glossary could be longer, as I kept running into words it didn't cover.

**95. Megan D. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Enjoyable, but the certifications section is too thin. I'd hoped for a clear step-by-step plan.

**96. Usman A. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
"How to Learn from This Book" is the best opening chapter I've seen. I'm actually finishing a tech book for once.

**97. Lily C. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
The crash course instructions skipped a step, and nothing worked until I found the fix online. Frustrating.

**98. Ethan W. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Arrived a week late but in good condition. Strong ideas, plain language, easy to dip into.

**99. Salma H. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
My favourite part was "Getting Paid", and it's only a few pages. I'd buy a whole book on that topic alone.

**100. Finn R. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Smart, readable and useful. The Preface and the Thesis together make a very strong start.

# Customer reviews: The AI Agent Factory, Second Edition (part 2 of 6)

Made-up practice data. These reviewers are not real people.

---

**21. Nadia J. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Really enjoyed the Thesis. My only gripe is the Glossary. I wanted it to cover more terms, as a few words used in the crash courses aren't in it.

**22. Liam G. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**
Ordered three weeks ago. It finally arrived with the shrink wrap ripped open and a dent in the cover. Not happy.

**23. Zara K. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
"Selling as a Vertical FDE" is gold. I used one of its ideas in a client call the same week. Arrived quickly and well packed.

**24. Ben A. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Interesting ideas, but the setup steps jump around. Step four mentions a file that step three never told me to create.

**25. Amara U. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
I loved "The Operating Layer". I'd have given five stars if there were a whole chapter on it rather than one essay.

**26. Pavel D. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
Shipping was promised in five days and took twelve. The book is good, but the delivery wasn't.

**27. Ellie R. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
Clear, calm writing. "How to Learn from This Book" stopped me from rushing ahead. I'm going chapter by chapter now and it's working.

**28. Samir H. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
The crash courses need clearer instructions for Windows users. Most of the examples felt written for a Mac.

**29. Olivia P. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Brilliant Preface. A bit too short on the certifications, though. I wanted more detail on what each one involves.

**30. Kenji M. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
I've read a lot of AI books. This one actually tells you what to do on Monday morning. Strongly recommend.

**31. Bella S. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**
The cover arrived scuffed and the corners were folded. It looked second-hand. Packaging needs work.

**32. Ade O. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
"Getting Paid" was my favourite section, and I wish it were twice as long. Still very helpful.

**33. Hannah C. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**
Good content, but I got lost in the setup instructions for the first crash course. A simple checklist at the start would help a lot.

**34. Arjun V. ★★★★★ (5/5)**
"What You Carry In" is the chapter I'll come back to most. It made me value my years of experience in a new way.

**35. Freya L. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**
Arrived a week late, but well packed. The Ecosystem section is a useful map of the field.

**36. Tariq Z. ★★☆☆☆ (2/5)**

**37. Ruby N. ★★★★★ (5/5)**

**38. Jonah B. ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

**39. Leila F. ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

**40. Connor W. ★☆☆☆☆ (1/5)**

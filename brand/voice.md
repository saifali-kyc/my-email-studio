# Brand voice guide: The AI Agent Factory, second edition

We write like a calm mentor talking to students. We teach first and sell second. Every email is under 250 words, teaches one idea, and has a clear call to action.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Calm**: steady and measured. No hype, no panic, no rush.
- **Mentoring**: we explain, give a small lesson, and guide the reader to the next step.
- **Plainspoken**: short sentences in plain English. If a term from the book needs explaining, we explain it.

## 2. Words we use

- **"We"**: we learn and build together. Use "we" and "you", rather than "the reader" or "users".
- Book terms, used exactly as written: **AI Workers**, **Five Horizons**, **AI Workforce**, **AI-Native Company**, **Autonomous Economy**, **Manage AI Workers. Build your own.**
- Words for learning: *learn, build, practise, chapter, step, guide, together*.
- Honest words for forecasts: *we expect, likely, may, our best guess*.
- The main call to action, word for word: **"Start reading free"**.

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Too casual**: slang, "hey guys", "super", "awesome", "lol", lots of exclamation marks, emoji.
- **Hype**: "revolutionary", "game-changer", "mind-blowing", "secret", "hack", "10x".
- **Pressure and fear of missing out**: "don't miss out", "last chance", "act now", "you'll be left behind".
- **Unexplained jargon**: any technical term we don't explain in the same email.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No job or income promises.** Never say the book will get someone a job, a raise or money.
- **No "AI will replace people".** Our message is the opposite: humans set purpose, define authority, and remain accountable.
- **No forecast stated as certain.** The Horizon dates are forecasts. Always hedge them ("we expect", "likely").
- **No unconfirmed facts.** Don't state the launch date, prices, certificates, the author byline or what's new in the second edition until they're confirmed in `brand-brief.md`.

## 5. How we sign off

- Put the call to action in two places: once near the top and once just before the sign-off. Each time, use the words **"Start reading free"**.
- Closing line: **See you in the next chapter,**
- From: **The Panaversity Team**

Example:

> See you in the next chapter,
> The Panaversity Team

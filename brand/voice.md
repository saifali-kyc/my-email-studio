# Brand voice guide: Rise & Crumb

Rise & Crumb is a small, made-up neighbourhood bakery. We write like a friendly baker chatting over the counter. Every email is under 150 words, shares one thing worth knowing, and has a clear call to action.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Warm**: kind and welcoming, like a regular who knows your order.
- **Cheerful**: light and happy, but never loud. One exclamation mark per email at most.
- **Simple**: short sentences in plain English. No fancy food jargon.

## 2. Words we use

- **"We"** and **"you"**: we bake, you enjoy.
- Baking words: *fresh, warm, golden, crusty, handmade, from scratch, this week*.
- The main call to action, word for word: **"See the spring menu"**.
- **British spelling**: *flavour, favourite, colour*.

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Hype**: "best ever", "life-changing", "insane", "to die for".
- **Pressure**: "last chance", "act now", "don't miss out", "selling fast".
- **Too casual**: slang, emoji, "lol", lots of exclamation marks.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No health claims.** Never say our bakes are healthy, good for you, low in sugar, gluten-free, guilt-free, or that they help with diets, energy or digestion.
- **No allergy promises.** Never say a bake is safe for any allergy.
- **No unconfirmed facts.** Don't state prices, opening hours, dates or offers unless they're confirmed in the campaign plan.

## 5. How we sign off

- Put the call to action in two places: once near the top and once just before the sign-off. Each time, use the words **"See the spring menu"**.
- Closing line: **Fresh from the oven,**
- From: **The Rise & Crumb Team**

Example:

> Fresh from the oven,
> The Rise & Crumb Team

Before we hand any work to an agent, we answer three questions. They come straight from the idea at the heart of the book: humans set purpose, define authority, and remain accountable.

**Try it with one task you repeat every week:**

1. **Purpose:** what is this task for? Write it in one sentence.
2. **Authority:** what may the agent do on its own, and what must it ask about first?
3. **Accountability:** who checks the result, and how?

If you can't answer all three, the task isn't ready to delegate yet. That is useful to know too.

**How this looks for you:**

- Try it as a class exercise, with each learner choosing their own task.

# Campaign plan: The AI Agent Factory, second edition

Awareness campaign by email only. Every email follows `brand/voice.md` and goes only to the practice email server.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Five Horizons
- **Summary:** Over four weeks, we send four short teaching emails. Each one explains one idea from the book and invites the reader to "Start reading free".
- **Main goal:** 2,000 clicks on "Start reading free" between Monday 9 November and Sunday 6 December 2026.
- **Other goals:**
  - Help each reader understand one idea from the book: the move from prompting chatbots to managing AI Workers.
  - Learn which of our four groups responds best, so we can plan the next campaign.

## 2. Target audience

We email all four groups, and students and career-switchers come first. They get each email one day before the other groups, and we write their version first.

| Order | Group (lead tag) | Who they are | What they want | What worries them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Students and career-switchers (`ai-curious`) | New to AI agents, building skills for their first role or a new career | A clear place to start, and skills they can practise | Feeling behind, and not knowing which skills matter |
| 2nd | Working professionals and team managers (`team-leads`) | People who will soon manage AI Workers alongside their teams | A practical way to delegate work to agents | Losing control of quality, and unclear responsibility |
| 3rd | Founders and business leaders (`business-leaders`) | People planning AI-native companies | A long-term picture to plan against | Betting on the wrong timeline |
| 4th | Educators and trainers (`educators`) | People who teach AI skills to others | A structure they can teach from | Material that dates quickly |

- **Where they spend time:** email, LinkedIn, online courses and learning communities. This campaign uses email only.
- **Buying stage:** awareness. Most readers don't know the second edition yet. The free reading link lets them try it at no cost.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** We are moving from prompting chatbots to managing AI Workers, and this book shows us how to take that step with care.

| Supporting message | Evidence from the book |
|---|---|
| 1. There is a clear path from querying software, to prompting chatbots, to delegating to agents, to managing AI Workers. | The Five Horizons map in the book (H0 to H4) |
| 2. Humans stay in charge. | The tagline "Across every horizon, humans set purpose, define authority, and remain accountable." |
| 3. Our future dates are honest forecasts, not promises. | Each horizon is given as a likely range, such as H1, the AI Worker era, 2026–2028. |
| 4. Anyone can start today, at no cost. | "Start reading free" at http://agentfactory2.panaversity.org/five-horizons/ |

**What each group hears first:**
- **Students and career-switchers:** "Here is where to start, one chapter at a time."
- **Team managers:** "Here is how we delegate to AI Workers and keep responsibility clear."
- **Founders:** "Here is a map of the next decade, with honest forecasts."
- **Educators:** "Here is a structure you can teach from."

## 4. Channel strategy

## 7. Success metrics

| Metric | Target | How we track it |
|---|---|---|
| **Clicks on "Start reading free" (main goal)** | **2,000 by Sun 6 Dec 2026 (about 500 per email)** | Tracking links per email and group |
| Click rate per email | Higher than the previous email | Clicks ÷ emails delivered |
| Clicks per group | Students and career-switchers lead | Group tag in each link |
| Open rate | Steady or rising week to week | Email tool report |
| Unsubscribe rate | Below 0.5% per email | Email tool report |
| Replies | Read and logged | Inbox |

- **Reporting:** a short check every Friday, plus a final report after Sun 6 Dec.
- **Practice note:** these emails go only to the practice server, so no real person clicks. The numbers above are the targets a real launch would track.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Our list is too small for the goal | 2,000 clicks from four emails means about 500 clicks per email. At a typical click rate of 3–5%, that needs a list of roughly 10,000 to 17,000 people. We don't know our list size yet. | Confirm the list size before 9 Nov. If it is smaller, lower the goal or add a second channel in the next campaign. |
| Sends near the US Thanksgiving holiday (Thu 26 Nov) | Fewer people may open emails in week 3 | Week 3 sends on Tue 24 and Wed 25 Nov, before the holiday. Watch the week 3 results closely. |
| Stating facts that aren't confirmed | `brand/voice.md` forbids stating the launch date, prices, certificates, the author byline or what's new in the second edition until they're confirmed | Leave these out. Run `/marketing:brand-review` on every email before sending. |
| One group stops opening | We lose part of our audience | Check on Fri 20 Nov and rewrite the subject lines or examples for that group. |

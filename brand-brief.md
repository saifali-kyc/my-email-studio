Use this brand in place of the sample "Kettlebird Tea / The Steep Club" in every prompt from email_studio.md.

- **Product:** The AI Agent Factory, second edition, by Panaversity
- **Book page:** http://agentfactory2.panaversity.org/five-horizons/
